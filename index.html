ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Place Where Every Child Can Shine

Chepkumia Joyland Academy, also known as Chepkumia Joyland Preparatory School or Joyland Comprehensive School, is a private educational institution located in the Chepkumia area near Kaimosi.

The school provides holistic academic and co-curricular learning while implementing the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC/CBE).
